Where to Find PayTo Agreements

  • Mobile Banking App: Most major Australian banks allow you to view, authorise, pause, or cancel PayTo agreements directly in their mobile banking apps. Look for a section called “PayTo Agreements” or similar, often under the “Payments” or “Settings” menu. They often send a notification on the mobile banking app.
  • Internet Banking (Web Browser): You can also access and manage PayTo agreements via your bank’s internet banking portal. The agreements are usually found under “Payments,” “Transfers,” or a dedicated “PayTo” section.

How to Authorise a PayTo Agreement

  1. Log in to your bank’s mobile app or internet banking.
  2. Navigate to the “PayTo Agreements” section.
  3. Review any new or pending agreements. These will typically appear at the top of the list or in a section marked “Action Required.”
  4. Select the agreement you wish to authorise.
  5. Check the details (amount, frequency, merchant, etc.) to ensure they are correct.
  6. Tap or click “Authorise” (or “Approve”). You may be asked to confirm your identity using a security code, app PIN, or biometric authentication.
  7. Confirmation will be shown once the agreement is active.

Mobile Banking or Internet Banking, which needs to be used?
Most banks support both: You can authorise and manage PayTo agreements in either the mobile app or internet banking. There is no need to visit a branch or use only one channel—choose whichever is more convenient for you.
